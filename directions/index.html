Directions

The 6th MLC Workshop is held as part of ICDM 2026 at the conference venue in Shenyang, China.

Workshop Location

  • Venue: Wanda Vista Shenyang (沈阳富力万达文华酒店)
  • Address: No. 17-5 Yingpan West Street, Hunnan District, Shenyang, Liaoning, China
  • Room: Kuala Lumpur
  • Date: Sunday, November 15, 2026 (ICDM 2026 Main Day 3)
  • Time: 13:30–15:30 and 15:50–18:00 (China Standard Time, UTC+8)
  • Conference registration desk: 1F, open 08:00–08:50
  • Lunch: 12:10–13:30, Grand Ballroom 3

Getting There

  • Nearest metro: Yingpan Street Station (Line 2), Exit B, about a 5-minute walk to the venue.
  • From Shenyang Taoxian International Airport: about 15–20 minutes by taxi, or Metro Line 2 to Yingpan Street Station.
  • From Shenyang North Railway Station: Metro Line 2 directly to Yingpan Street Station, or about 25 minutes by taxi.
  • From Shenyang Railway Station / Shenyang South Railway Station: transfer to Metro Line 2 for Yingpan Street Station, or about 25–35 minutes by taxi.

Full directions are available on the official ICDM 2026 Venue page.

Accommodation and Visa

  • Official hotel: the conference venue, Wanda Vista Shenyang. See Official Hotel for booking (booking closes October 31, 2026) and Recommended Hotels for other options.
  • Visa: see the ICDM 2026 Visa Information page.

Registration

Registration for the MLC workshop is handled through the main conference. Please register on the ICDM 2026 Registration System. See also the ICDM 2026 Author Instructions.

  • Registration is mandatory for accepted papers. At least one author of each accepted paper must register for ICDM 2026. Papers without a registration have been removed from the ICDM workshop proceedings and the workshop program.
  • For questions about a registration or payment, please contact the ICDM 2026 Registration Team at icdm2026@iccmacao.com.
  • For questions about the MLC workshop, please contact the workshop organizers listed on the home page.
